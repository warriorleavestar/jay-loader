   may refuse any application, and does not need to give a reason.

3. **Own use only.** The applicant must be both the end user of Jay Loader and the

   sublicensed.

4. **Profit limit.** The permission covers use only while the real profit
   from Jay Loader stays below US$250,000 in each 12-month period. Real profit
   means the actual increase in assets, and the actual increase in the
   value of assets, that the applicant's project brings to its
   shareholders and beneficiaries as a direct or indirect result of Jay Loader.
   The licensor may agree to a different limit in the written consent.

5. **Going over the limit.** If real profit reaches the limit, the applicant
   must tell the licensor within 30 days. The permission ends 30 days after
   the limit is reached, unless the licensor agrees in writing to a new
   limit or a separate commercial license.

6. **Transfer.** This permission, and any license or consent the licensor
